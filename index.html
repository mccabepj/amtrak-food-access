Assumptions and limits

Scores describe a fixed three-route study, not live conditions or individual passenger journeys. Nearby access uses straight-line estimates. Opening hours, platform access, permission to alight or sell food, demand and timetable feasibility are unverified.

The platform scenario assumes a six-minute window. The longer-stop scenario tests a minimum 30-minute TOTAL stop, not 30 extra minutes. It shows improvement in estimated access, not just usefulness after extending the stop.
